01 — About

Stories cut
with spirit.

I’m Ujal Rai, professionally known as UZaL, a student at Gedu Higher Secondary School in Bhutan with a passion for videography and video editing. I enjoy transforming raw footage into unique, creative, and meaningful visual stories that reflect my vision and creativity. My dream is to represent Bhutan on the international stage and work towards making my creations recognized globally. I believe in the philosophy of **Kaizen — continuous improvement** — and strive to learn, grow, and refine my skills with every project I create. I know I still have a long way to go, but I am committed to pushing my creative boundaries, exploring new ideas, and becoming a better creator every day. Above all, I aspire to make my country and my parents proud through my work and dedication. Thank you for visiting my website and being part of my creative journey. I hope you enjoy my work, and I’m always open to new opportunities, meaningful projects, and creative collaborations. **Let’s create something extraordinary together.**
